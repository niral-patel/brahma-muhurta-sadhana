    Suggestions

    • Move your bedtime first. A 3:30 AM start needs you in bed by 8:30 to 9:00 PM. Without that the routine will not last a week.
    • Eat light and early. Finish dinner 2 to 3 hours before bed, and put screens away for the last 30 minutes.
    • Prepare the night before. Lay out the mat, clothes and puja thali, and keep a glass of water by the bed.
    • Practise on an empty stomach. Warm water on waking is enough. Wait 20 to 30 minutes after the routine before breakfast.
    • Same place, same time. Sit facing east or north in a clean, quiet corner you use every day.
    • Keep the order. Exercise first, then the shower, then pranayama, then meditation, then puja. Each one settles the body for the next.
    • Start small. In week one, switch off some steps and do 30 minutes. Add one step a week.
    • Surya Namaskar. Begin with 3 or 4 rounds and add one round a week, up to 12.
    • Rest between pranayamas. Take five normal breaths before the next one. If you have time, switch on Shavasana after Ujjayi.
    • Silence the phone. Use Do Not Disturb so only this app can interrupt you.
    • The real Brahma muhurta moves with the sunrise. It runs from two night-muhurtas before sunrise to one before, roughly 96 to 48 minutes. The Panchang tab shows today's time for Dallas.
    • Consistency beats length. On a short-sleep day, do a 20-minute version instead of skipping.
