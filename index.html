    Suggestions · સૂચનો

    • Move your bedtime first. A 3:30 AM start needs you in bed by 8:30 to 9:00 PM. Without that the routine will not last a week.
    • Eat light and early. Finish dinner 2 to 3 hours before bed, and put screens away for the last 30 minutes.
    • Prepare the night before. Lay out the mat, clothes, mala, book and puja thali, and keep a glass of water by the bed.
    • Practise on an empty stomach. Warm water on waking is enough. Wait 20 to 30 minutes after the routine before breakfast.
    • Same place, same time. Sit facing east or north in a clean, quiet corner you use every day.
    • Keep the order. Exercise first, then the shower, then pranayama, meditation, japa, puja and reading. Each one settles the body for the next.
    • Start small. Use the 20 or 30 minute length for the first weeks, then move to Full.
    • Surya Namaskar. Begin with 3 or 4 rounds and add one round a week, up to 12. Say one of the twelve names of Surya at the start of each round.
    • Rama nama. Keep one mantra for japa and stay with it. Count on a mala, or tap the circle in the Japa step and let the app count to 108.
    • Read a little every day. Ten slow minutes of the Gita or the Ramayana is better than an hour once a week. Note the page so the tracker shows your progress.
    • The real Brahma muhurta moves with the sunrise. It runs from two night-muhurtas before sunrise to one before, roughly 96 to 48 minutes. The Panchang tab shows today's time for your location.
    • Consistency beats length. On a short-sleep day, do the 20 minute version instead of skipping. The Progress tab keeps your streak, year by year.

    How to do each step, with shlokas

    The shlokas are the commonly recited versions. Wording varies a little between traditions, so follow your own family's or guru's version where it differs.

    Meditation through the week · ધ્યાન

    Care: skip Kapalbhati and Bhastrika if you are pregnant, have high blood pressure, heart disease, a hernia or recent abdominal surgery. Ease into cold showers if you have a heart condition. Stop any practice that makes you dizzy and breathe normally. This is a guide, not medical advice.

    Progress · પ્રગતિ

    A day is marked when you finish the routine after at least 5 minutes of practice. Tap any past day to mark or unmark it by hand. Every year is kept; use the arrows to look back.
